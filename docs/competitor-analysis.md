# Competitor Feature Analysis: AI Mental Health Support

Note: figures and claims for Shezlong and Wysa are company-reported (press release and website).

## 1. Woebot Health

| Feature | Details |
|---|---|
| Expert-written conversations | Dialogues are authored by conversational writers with clinical experts. NLP interprets user input and selects a suitable pre-built technique, rather than generating therapy freely. |
| Concerning-language detection | NLP flags potentially risky language and immediately offers information about external help resources. Woebot states it is not a crisis service. |
| Clinical-grade privacy | All user data is treated as Protected Health Information under HIPAA, even when not legally required. Sensitive data is held in a dedicated environment and audited annually by an external party. |
| No data selling | User data is not shared or sold for advertising. |
| Safety governance | A Safety Assessment Committee of clinical, regulatory, and medical experts reviews trial and post-market data. |
| Research ethics | Studies are IRB-reviewed, trials are registered, and protocols and results are published. |
| Age restriction | Requires confirmation that the user is 18 or older. |

## 2. Shezlong AI Therapy Assistant

Source: Wamda press release, 29 April 2025.

| Feature | Details |
|---|---|
| Therapist support tool | Assists therapists, not replaces them, with insights and analytics on each patient's psychological state. |
| Personalised treatment reports | Auto-generated reports per patient. |
| Emotional pattern analysis | Tracks emotional patterns and progress milestones over time. |
| Therapist-patient matching | Suggests the best-suited therapist based on emotional and cognitive inputs. |
| Between-session companion | Personalised self-care activities, emotional check-ins, and cognitive exercises that adapt to the user's state. |
| Privacy and consent | Described as privacy-first and HIPAA-aligned, with consent built into each step of the AI process. |

## 3. Wysa

| Feature | Details |
|---|---|
| Fully anonymous | No stigma, designed to feel safe and judgment-free. |
| Always available | No appointments or waiting. |
| Human in the loop | All care pathways include human oversight. |
| Safer by design | AI does not make decisions on its own; support is explainable and fits clinical workflows. |
| Multiple care pathways | Self-help, guided referral, between-session care, and tools for children and young people. |
| Local adaptation (Dreamkit) | Physical workbooks plus digital tools, delivered in Marathi for adolescent girls in rural India. |
| Clear crisis disclaimer | Not designed for crises or medical advice; directs users to country-specific emergency hotlines. |
| Research backing | Cites 45+ peer-reviewed publications. |
| Age policy | 18+, with parental review advised for ages 13 to 18. |
